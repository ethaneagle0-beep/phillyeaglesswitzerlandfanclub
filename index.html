Our story

From Philly Sundays
to Swiss Sundays.

We're a Father and Son who have grown to love the Eagles while living in Switzerland — chasing a stream at odd hours, crazy layovers to get to Philadelphia, building a jersey collection one player at a time. We started this fan club because we know we can't be the only Eagles fans in Switzerland. Whether you're Swiss, American, or from anywhere else, we want to bring Eagles fans together to watch games, make new friends, and grow a community that feels a little closer to Philadelphia on game day.
